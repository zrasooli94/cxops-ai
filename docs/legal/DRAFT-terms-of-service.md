## 1. The service

CXOps AI provides a website chat assistant, a conversation inbox with human
handoff, team workspaces, and usage reporting. During early access, features
may change and some are not yet available (self-service sign-up, online
billing, automatic invitation emails).

## 2. Accounts and access

* Workspaces are created by CXOps after a reviewed request; access is by
  invitation to a verified email address.
* The customer is responsible for who it invites and for the roles it assigns.
* CXOps may suspend a workspace that `[[LEGAL: grounds — e.g. breach, abuse,
  non-payment, security risk]]`. Suspension stops the assistant immediately.

## 3. Customer content and AI

* The customer provides the knowledge the assistant uses and is responsible for
  its accuracy and for having the right to use it.
* AI-generated answers can be incorrect or incomplete. The customer remains
  responsible for its communications with its own customers; human handoff is
  provided so staff can take over.
* Customer content is processed by the sub-processors listed in the Privacy
  Policy, including OpenAI for AI replies.

## 4. Usage limits and fees

* Each workspace has a monthly AI allowance (conversations and an *estimated*
  cost ceiling). When reached, messages are routed to the customer's team
